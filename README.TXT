PROJECT TITLE: The King's Gambit

PURPOSE OF PROJECT: 2025 AP Comp Sci Freshman Year End Project

PROJECT DESCRIPTION: 
- This game is a recreation of chess. Two players control the white and black pieces. 
- To win, you must checkmate the opponent's king. 
- The special moves are all possible, including castling, en passant, and promotion.

VERSION or DATE: v1.0.0 (June 2025)

HOW TO START THIS PROJECT: 
- Download the project and open it in Greenfoot, or visit https://www.greenfoot.org/scenarios/36674
